Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker is an offline-first app. This page describes how data is handled in your browser.

1) Data We Collect

  • Habit data (habit names, targets, completion history, streaks, XP) is stored in your browser’s localStorage and never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign in.
  • When you use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes your email, display name, and Google account ID. A copy of your email and display name is stored locally in your browser’s localStorage to personalize the app.
  • No habit data is sent to any server. All habit tracking data remains in your browser.

2) How We Use Data

We use local data to:

  • Display your habit checklist and history
  • Calculate progress and streaks (weekly/monthly views)
  • Calculate XP/levels based on your current habit statuses
  • If signed in: personalize the account section with your name and email

3) Cookies

The app does not use cookies for its own functionality. Firebase Authentication may use session cookies as part of its authentication flow when you sign in with Google.

4) Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — handles Google Sign-In when you choose to sign in. Firebase receives your Google account credentials during sign-in.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) — collects anonymous usage analytics. No personal information is sent to analytics.
  • Firebase Firestore — the SDK is loaded but is not actively used to store or sync your habit data.

No third-party service receives your habit names, targets, completion history, or any other tracking data.

5) Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third party. Firebase Authentication and Google Analytics are the only services that receive any data, and only as described above.

6) Your Choices

You can:

  • Use the app in Guest mode without signing in — no personal data is collected.
  • Delete all data at any time using the app’s Delete all option in Settings, or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Sign out from Settings to remove stored authentication information from localStorage.
  • Clear browser site data to remove all locally stored information.
