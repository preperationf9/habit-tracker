Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker is an offline-first app. This page describes how data is handled in your browser.

1) Data We Collect

  • Nothing is sent to a server. The app works locally.
  • Habit data (habit names, targets, and your history for each day) is stored in your browser’s localStorage.

2) How We Use Data

We use local data to:

  • Display your habit checklist and history
  • Calculate progress and streaks
  • Calculate XP/levels based on your current habit statuses

3) Cookies

The app does not use cookies. Your data is stored in localStorage.

4) Third-Party Services

There are no third-party services used by this app to collect your personal information.

5) Your Choices

You can delete your data at any time using the app’s Delete all option, or by clearing site data in your browser.

6) Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer of this app.
